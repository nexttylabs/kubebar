| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-07-04 | Tampering | UAT and generated evidence | mitigate | Generator writes temp output; UAT is updated deliberately by the executor and does not get overwritten by the gate. |
| T-07-05 | Information Disclosure | screenshots and docs | mitigate | Screenshot paths are standardized under `docs/assets/qa/`; evidence rules forbid raw transcripts, tokens, kubeconfig paths, full JSON, and sensitive cluster details. |
</threat_model>
